WHO WE ARE
       
         

Better healthcare starts with working together.

         

            Health Care Sierra Leone USA brings people, professional expertise             and partnerships together in support of improved healthcare in             Sierra Leone.          

         

            Through healthcare volunteerism, professional mentorship, education             and collaboration with local institutions, HCSL-USA seeks to help             strengthen healthcare capacity and support more accessible, quality             care.
